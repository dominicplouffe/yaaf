Spacing
4-pt base. Inside a card 12–16; between cards 16–22; between zones 24. Zones are separated by space, not by more borders.
Containers
Three, by role: card (a thing), decision card (indigo ring — the only ringed object), sunken (supporting detail). No card-in-card.
Motion
160ms ease-out for state chips changing; a decided card collapses into its confirmation (240ms); a newly verified node fills once. Nothing loops. prefers-reduced-motion → instant.
Words
Headline ≤ 8 words · reason ≤ 20 · "Why?" opens the rest. No snake_case, no raw limits, no ISO dates, no sentence shown twice on one screen.
