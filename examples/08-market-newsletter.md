# 08 — A newsletter that knows if anyone read it

**You want:** weekly commentary that strangers choose to read. **You don't
want:** 20 posts and a subscriber count made entirely of you.

**Import:** `08-market-newsletter.json`. **Edit `controlled_domains`** before
you start — put your own newsletter domain and `github:your-account` in it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Shape | **Operate** — a weekly cycle, rolling Wednesdays |
| Teams | Editorial (`finance-writer`), Research (`equity-analyst`), Compliance (`compliance-officer`) |
| Budget | $250/month |
| Notable | `require_evidence_verification: true` · `external_ratio_min: 0.5` |

## Controlled domains are the whole point

Anything on that list **cannot count as external evidence**. Your own site
reporting your own success is not proof. Which is why, when the Work tab does
show you three inbound links, they are three strangers.

With verification on, an agent-claimed source is fetched, hashed and
snapshotted by the platform before it counts. A dead link counts for nothing —
and the drought clock keeps running while the company looks busy. That is the
mechanism that stops "we published four posts" from reading as progress.

## Compliance is a gate in the pipeline

Nothing ships without a PASS. The reviewer checks the things that actually
matter for published market commentary:

- Is this general research, or does it read as personalised advice?
- Are hypothetical or backtested results labelled as such, with assumptions?
- Does every material claim have a public source, licensed to use this way?
- Are positions disclosed? Is anything here promotional about a name the desk
  has a view on?

`ESCALATE` means a real legal question and it comes to you — the reviewer is
explicitly not a lawyer and will not pretend to be one. Tell it your
jurisdiction in the boardroom on day one; it will not guess.

## The bet, and its kill date

Expect the CIO to state something like:

> **H1:** Weekly deconstructions of one filing find an audience among people
> who read filings. **Kill if** by day 60 unaffiliated subscribers have not
> grown and no post has earned an inbound link from an account we do not
> control.

At day 60 you either double down or change the format. "Post harder" is not on
the menu, and the goal says so out loud.

## Your job

Press publish, and do the distribution. It is the part agents are worst at and
you are best at.
