# 07 — Macro, translated into consequences

**You want:** to know what the macro picture means for what you hold. **You
don't want:** a weekly essay about the economy.

**Import:** `07-macro-watch.json`. Set your timezone — the cycle rolls Mondays
in it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Shape | **Operate** — a weekly cycle, rolling Mondays |
| Teams | Macro (`macro-analyst` ×2), Tape (`market-news-analyst`) |
| Budget | $250/month · review every 14 days |
| Notable | `memory_cap_project: 80` |

## The rule that makes it worth reading

**Read what the market already prices first.** Forward curves, breakevens,
swap-implied policy paths, the shape of the curve. Say what they are pricing,
*then* say where you differ and why. "The market expects three cuts by June; I
think one, because X" is analysis. "Rates will fall" is not.

Every view carries a horizon, the mechanism that has to play out in order, the
observable that would falsify it, and a probability the analyst is willing to
be scored on.

## The scorecard is the deliverable that compounds

The role maintains a file of past calls, what happened, and what it got wrong,
updated whenever a call resolves. A desk that does not track its own hit rate
is guessing with extra steps — and after a quarter this file is worth more than
any individual note.

Ask for it at the review. If the hit rate is 50% on binary calls, that is
useful information about how much weight to put on the next one.

## Vintages

Macro data is revised. The prompt requires the vintage used to be stated and
the conclusion checked against the revision history. A view built on a print
that was later revised away is not a view.

## Memory, raised on purpose

`memory_cap_project` is 80 rather than 60: a macro desk accumulates durable
facts (this central bank's reaction function, this series' revision pattern)
that stay true for quarters. Caps are refusals here, never silent eviction —
when it fills, the CIO compacts, and folded entries stay readable.

## What to watch

- **Weak links, stated as weak.** Most macro variables explain very little of
  next quarter's equity returns. A desk that says so when it is true is doing
  the job.
- **Identity vs correlation vs story.** The prompt asks for the distinction.
  Check that the note makes it.
